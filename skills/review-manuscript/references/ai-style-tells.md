# AI style tells to avoid

Edits must not introduce these patterns.
If the original text already contains them, leave them unless the sentence is unclear.

## Vocabulary

Avoid words and phrases that are overused by language models, for example:
delve, crucial, pivotal, intricate, seamless, robust (when not statistical), comprehensive, landscape,
realm, tapestry, underscore, showcase, leverage (as a verb), harness, foster, notably, furthermore (in series),
"it is worth noting that", "plays a crucial role", "in the realm of", "a testament to", "paves the way".

## Structure

- No em dashes used as a stylistic device; keep the author's punctuation.
- No bullet lists or bold text inserted into prose.
- No triads of adjectives or "not only... but also" added for rhythm.
- No sentence made longer, more formal or more emphatic than the original.
- No summary or concluding sentence added at the end of a paragraph.
- No hedging or enthusiasm that the author did not express.

## Principle

A good edit is short, local and invisible: a reader should not be able to tell which sentences were edited.
